# Elison's World — Community Building Plan

## Phase 1: Foundation (Week 1)

### 1.1 Discord Server "Elison's World"
**Structure:**
- #welcome — Rules, roles, how to enter
- #the-clearing — Game discussion, puzzle solving, fragment hunting
- #new-music — Early drops, exclusive previews, listening parties
- #the-molecule — Fan stories, "nothing was random" moments
- #prayer-room — Spiritual space, vulnerable shares (moderated)
- #behind-the-world — BTS, process, studio photos
- #meetups — Local cells (Miami, NYC, DR)
- #feedback — Direct input on new music

**Roles:**
- @Founder (you)
- @Moderator (trusted fans)
- @Inner Circle (early access tier)
- @Fragment Hunter (game progress)
- @Cell Leader (local host)

### 1.2 Email Tier System
**The Inner Circle:**
- Sign up on site → immediate welcome email
- New music 48h before public
- Monthly "Fragment" email with story + exclusive
- Direct reply channel (monitored by you)

**The Outer World:**
- Public social, standard release schedule
- No early access

### 1.3 "Your Fragment" Submission System
**What:**
- Fans submit 100-500 word stories
- Theme: "Nothing was random. It was all connected."
- Best stories featured on site in "The Molecule" section
- Monthly winner gets personal video from you

**How:**
- Form on site (/fragment/)
- Discord bot for submissions
- Curated by you, published weekly

## Phase 2: Engagement (Weeks 2-4)

### 2.1 Game Expansion
**Community-unlocked rooms:**
- 100 Discord members → unlock "Love" room
- 500 → unlock "Faith" room
- 1000 → unlock "Pain" room
- 5000 → full molecule (all 8 rooms)

### 2.2 Monthly "Enter the World" Sessions
**Format:**
- 1 hour on Discord voice
- You play unreleased material (acoustic, demos)
- Q&A, not interview
- Intimate, not performative
- Recorded for Inner Circle only

### 2.3 Collaborative Playlist "The Connection"
**How:**
- Spotify playlist fans can add to
- Theme: songs that healed/connected them
- You curate monthly, share back with commentary
- Becomes community soundtrack

## Phase 3: Scale (Months 2-3)

### 3.1 Local Cells
**Structure:**
- Fans apply to host listening sessions
- You provide: exclusive audio, talking points, merch
- They host: 10-20 people, intimate space
- Report back to community

### 3.2 "Pass the Light" Mentorship
**How:**
- Community nominates emerging artists
- You select 1 per quarter
- Studio time, guidance, feature on your platform
- Documented for community

### 3.3 Merch Drop
**Items:**
- Everything Molecule pendant (hexagon, black gold)
- "Nothing was random" tee
- "Elison's World" candle (scent: night, rain, smoke)
- Limited, numbered

## Anti-Patterns (Avoid)
- Over-promising content schedules
- Generic "fan club" feel
- Losing mysterious/spiritual tone
- Too much automation (keep it personal)
- Sponsored content

## Tone Guide
- Vulnerable, not polished
- Spiritual, not preachy
- Mysterious, not cryptic
- Personal, not corporate
- Healing, not selling

## Metrics to Track
- Discord active members (weekly)
- Email open rates (Inner Circle)
- Fragment submissions (monthly)
- Game room unlocks (community size)
- Local cell applications (quarterly)
